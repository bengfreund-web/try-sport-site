Our Programs

TRY Rugby

TRY Rugby is an eight-week non-contact rugby curriculum, complemented by on-site clinics and all the equipment, provided free to your school. It's the first TRY Sport program, introducing students to the fastest-growing sport in Montana.

Bring TRY Rugby to Your School

How It Works

What Your School Gets

Free to Schools

Curriculum, clinics, and equipment are all provided at no cost to your school.

Rugby in a Box

We send everything, the full curriculum, rugby balls, and all the equipment you need to run the program.

On-Site Clinics

MIS instructors can deliver the program directly with your staff and students.

Non-Contact & Inclusive

Safe, non-contact, and open to every student, breaking down barriers to play.

Why It Matters

More Than a New Sport

TRY Rugby offers a non-contact, welcoming pathway into rugby, the fastest-growing sport in the state of Montana. It's easy to pick up and open to every student from the very first whistle.

Along the way, students build teamwork, confidence, and resilience, getting active in a way that supports mental health alongside physical and social growth for young student-athletes.

Proven Results

The Game Is Growing Fast in Montana

Youth club programs have doubled, and two high-school programs have earned national rankings.
